# Livi Creative and Usage Guidelines

[日本語](livi-guidelines.ja.md) | **English**

These guidelines apply to the [official Livi sample](../samples/v0.1.0/livi/livi.livf) and its internal material. The v0.1.0 license and additional permissions are also stated in the [external license document](../samples/v0.1.0/livi/livi.livf.license.txt) next to the file. See the [samples README](../samples/README.md) for the sample layout.

## About Livi

Livi is the mascot character of the LIVF project. We want LIVF users and creators to be able to make and publish works featuring Livi freely.

These guidelines aim to preserve **Livi's origin as a mascot created for LIVF** and **the distinction between official LIVF project releases and other works**. They do not fix Livi's personality, setting, role in a story, or form of expression.

## What you can do

You may use official Livi material or create works featuring Livi without prior permission. For example, you may:

- Feature Livi in illustrations, comics, videos, games, streams, websites, apps, educational materials, and merchandise.
- Change Livi's expressions, clothing, appearance, setting, personality, name, world, or relationships with other characters.
- Extract, edit, adapt, and convert the material to other formats.
- Publish, distribute, or sell the original material or modified works, whether free of charge or for a fee.

These guidelines impose no additional restrictions on creative genres, content, or production methods. You may feature Livi in works that do not use the LIVF format or otherwise relate directly to LIVF. The LIVF project does not review content before publication.

## Development, research, and education

When the **primary purpose** of using official Livi material or a modified version is to implement or validate the LIVF format, check interoperability, test, benchmark, conduct research, teach, or create technical documentation or presentations, the rights holder waives the attribution and notice requirements in Section 3(a)(1) of CC BY 4.0, whether the use is paid or free of charge, commercial or noncommercial.

This waiver includes parser and renderer tests, CI fixtures, test data in public repositories, sample inputs distributed with SDKs, libraries, or tools, format conversion tests, demo screens explaining features, and images in papers, technical articles, or presentation materials. Livi appearing on a demo screen or the software being paid does not, by itself, exclude the use from this waiver.

The technical and research waiver does not apply to creative uses, such as games, videos, or merchandise, that use Livi as a character or as the subject of viewing or sale. If you use Livi both to explain technology and in a creative work, check the ordinary attribution conditions for the publication or distribution of the creative work.

## A general-purpose CC0 sample

We also plan to provide a `.livf` sample that does not contain Livi, is available under CC0, and can be used to check basic features of the LIVF format. Livi can be used for technical and research purposes without attribution even in commercial settings, but you can choose the CC0 sample if you prefer not to assess whether a use qualifies for a waiver. This sample has not been published yet.

## Distinguishing official works

Works featuring Livi made by individuals or organizations are their creators' works. Only works that the LIVF project explicitly publishes as official are treated as official LIVF works.

You may describe the origin of the material with phrases such as “a work using Livi” or “fan art of Livi, the LIVF mascot.” Please avoid presenting your own work as “official LIVF” or “approved by LIVF,” or otherwise implying that the project created, approved, or sponsored it. Using the LIVF name or official logo as an official identifier for your own product or organization is a separate matter from the license for Livi material.

Use of official material or these guidelines does not mean that the LIVF project has approved or endorsed the content of your work.
